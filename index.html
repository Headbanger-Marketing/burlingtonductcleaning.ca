FAQs

Straight Answers, No Guesswork

The calls we get every week, answered the way we answer them on the phone.

How often do Burlington homes need duct cleaning?

Every two to three years is the norm. Post-war ducting that has never been cleaned should start with one thorough pass, then settle into the cycle. Renovations, pets, and dust from escarpment roads argue for the shorter end of that range.

Are your people certified and insured?

Both, on every job. The province issues no credential specific to this trade, so the meaningful assurances are technician certification, ongoing training, and full liability coverage, and we bring all three.

Do you show proof of the cleaning?

Yes. The inspection camera goes down the trunk lines before and after, and both rolls play on our screen before we leave. You keep copies of the images.

How can I tell the dryer vent is the problem?

The classic pattern is heat without progress: loads warm but stay damp, the laundry room turns muggy, and the outside cap barely flutters. We clear the route and measure the flow, and if the appliance itself is at fault we say so plainly.

What does a cleaning visit involve, and how long?

Negative pressure established at the furnace, registers removed and washed, each branch brushed under suction, the blower compartment cleaned, and camera documentation at the end. Most Burlington homes run three to four hours.

The vents smell when the season changes. Can you help?

That odour is residue on the duct surfaces. A full cleaning removes most of it, and a sanitizer treatment applied to the clean system handles the remainder. Treatment without cleaning just perfumes the problem.

Why is the house dusty even though we clean constantly?

In housing of this vintage the usual culprit is leaking return ductwork pulling basement air into the system. We pressure-check for exactly that and seal what we find.

Where do you work beyond Burlington itself?

Across Oakville, Milton, Georgetown, Waterdown, Hamilton, and Ancaster, plus the rural properties in between. Call with your address if you sit near the edge of the map.

Do you serve areas outside of Burlington, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Burlington and nearby communities including Oakville, Hamilton, Ancaster, Milton, and the surrounding area.
